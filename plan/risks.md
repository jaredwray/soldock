# Risk register

Reviewed at every stage gate. **L**ikelihood and **I**mpact are rated H/M/L. A risk is closed when
its trigger has passed or the mitigation is in place and verified.

| ID | Risk | L | I | Mitigation | Trigger / owner task | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| R-01 | **Outbound mail.** Cloudflare Email Service is in beta and positioned for transactional mail. Person-to-person mailbox use may conflict with its terms or quotas. Limits are 5 MiB per message, 50 recipients, and a daily quota that grows with reputation. **There is no Cloudflare-only fallback** (no outbound port 25). | M | H | Viability spike and a written acceptable-use question to Cloudflare **in S00** (S00.T13). The answer gates the S04 kickoff, and a negative answer triggers the [contingency](vision.md#contingency-outbound-mail). Oversized attachments become `/dl/` links (S05.T21), later Drive links (S06.T12). Split recipient lists into batches. Monitor quotas and ramp volume gradually. Request limit increases early. Internal-to-internal delivery never uses the service. | S00.T13, S05.T01 | open |
| R-02 | **No IMAP/SMTP client access.** Workers cannot accept raw inbound TCP, so desktop and mobile mail clients cannot connect. | H | M | Stated as a non-goal. Provide a strong PWA. Put JMAP over HTTPS on the backlog. | vision | accepted |
| R-03 | **Deliverability** from shared Cloudflare sending infrastructure (reputation, blocklists). | M | H | DKIM, SPF and DMARC at onboarding. Suppression lists. Bounce and complaint handling. Agent rate limits. A test suite with seed accounts at major providers. Gradual volume ramp. | S05.T04, S05.T12 | open |
| R-04 | **Durable Object memory** (128 MB per isolate) for large CRDT documents and spreadsheets. | M | H | Benchmarks in S07.T01 and S09.T01. Compaction. Lazy-loaded sheets. Size limits with clear errors. Split very large workbooks per sheet. | S07.T01, S09.T01 | open |
| R-05 | **Spreadsheet engine.** The best JavaScript engine (HyperFormula) is GPL. Excel fidelity is a deep problem. | H | M | Evaluate IronCalc (MIT/Apache, Rust → WASM) or a custom engine on formulajs. Define the supported function list and fidelity targets in S09.T01. Use conformance tests. | S09.T01 | open |
| R-06 | **Office format fidelity** (DOCX, XLSX, PPTX import and export). | H | M | Set clear expectations (best effort, "round-trip safe for common content"). Build a corpus of real files with visual regression tests. A Containers-based converter is the escape hatch. | S08.T04, S09.T07, S10.T06 | open |
| R-07 | **Meeting recording.** There is no native composite recorder on the bare SFU. RealtimeKit's core SDK ships only compiled code. | M | M | Spike S11.T12 compares a Containers recorder, RealtimeKit and client-side recording. Captions and transcripts through the WebSocket adapter can ship independently. | S11.T12 | open |
| R-08 | **Prompt injection against agent principals.** Inbound mail or shared docs could instruct agents to exfiltrate data. | H | H | Untrusted-content envelopes. | R-09 | **Same-origin user content** leading to XSS. | M | H | Never serve active content inline. Sandbox CSP. Sanitizer allowlist. Strict app CSP. Tests in S01.T12 and S04.T06. Review in S13.T03. | S01.T12 | open |
| R-10 | **Backup immutability.** R2 bucket locks can be removed by anyone with bucket access. | M | H | Disaster-recovery copies go to a separate Cloudflare account with object-scoped write tokens. | R-11 | **D1 read replication and Secrets Store are still beta.** | M | L | Design so the system works without replicas (cache auth lookups). Fall back to Worker secrets. | S01.T01 | open |
| R-12 | **Directory hot spot.** D1 handles every authenticated request. | L | M | Cache sessions and addresses with short TTLs. Use the Sessions API with replicas. Load-test in S13. | S13.T01 | open |

| R-14 | **Platform limits change** (pricing, limits, product renames). | M | M | Read the Cloudflare changelog at every gate. Keep a cost model (S13.T07). Isolate each product behind a `platform/` adapter. | each gate | open |
| R-15 | **Scope size.** Six products is a very large surface for one team of humans and agents. | H | H | Ship stages as vertical slices. | R-16 | **Cost at scale** (Durable Object duration, Workers AI minutes, R2 storage, Realtime egress). | M | M | Hibernation everywhere. Transcription is opt-in. Lifecycle rules move old data to Infrequent Access. S13.T07 builds the cost model. | S13.T07 | open |
| R-17 | **Backup scale.** About 1M objects per night, a 6 h window, Workflow step and result limits, and GC over millions of blobs. | M | H | Dirty-set incrementals plus a staggered weekly full. | R-18 | **Mailbox size.** Text plus FTS may reach 10–20 KB per message. A long Gmail history could approach the 10 GB limit per object. | M | H | Measure bytes per message in S04.T01 and set a size guard with a clear error. Decide at S04 kickoff whether date-based mailbox sharding moves from the backlog into S12 for imports. | S04.T01 | open |
| R-19 | **Browser Run cost.** It is billed per concurrent browser above the included 10 (about $2 each, monthly average of daily peaks). Rendering thumbnails on every edit would scale cost with editing. | M | M | UI thumbnails are rendered in the browser. Browser Run is reserved for PDF export and agent slide images, cached by content hash. Keep UI-flow load-test concurrency small. | S10.T07, S13.T01 | open |
| R-20 | **Email Routing limits.** Catch-all works only on apex domains. Subdomains are capped at 200 per-address rules, and a zone allows 30 mail domains. | H | M | Development uses the project's apex zone, `soldock.org` (R-23). Rules for non-apex domains are managed through the API, with the cap surfaced (S04.T02). Production's `jaredwray.com` is an apex zone. | S00.T09, S04.T02 | open |
| R-21 | **Production before backups.** Production runs from M2 (S05.T22), but the backup system is deferred to S12. Until then, bugs and accidental or malicious deletions are covered only by Cloudflare's durability and native 30-day point-in-time recovery. **Account compromise is not covered at all until S12.T17** (the disaster-recovery copy in a separate account). | M | H | A deliberate choice: build the core first. Interim measures: an admin `restore_to_time` operation plus the `docs/operations/pitr.md` runbook for Durable Object and D1 restores, rehearsed on development (S05.T24); blob GC grace of at least 45 days so restored rows find their bytes (S03.T08); soft-delete and trash everywhere; no destructive bulk operation without `dry_run`. Pull S12.T13–T17 forward if data loss would be unacceptable before then. | S05.T22, S05.T24, S12.T17 | accepted |
| R-22 | **CI holds the only Cloudflare credentials** ([ADR-0015](adr/0015-cloudflare-credentials-only-in-github-actions.md)). A compromised third-party action, a malicious dependency installed in a deploy job, a direct push to an unprotected `main`, or a workflow change that adds an unsafe trigger could exfiltrate the development or production token. A development leak also reaches the public domain of the project (R-23). | L | H | `main` is protected before the first environment secret exists (S00.T09). Environment secrets are limited to `main`, and production also needs a reviewer. The maintainer reads each workflow change against the ADR-0015 trigger allowlist before the merge, and reads each workflow already on `main` before the first secret exists (S00.T09). SHA-pinned actions, Socket Firewall on `--frozen-lockfile` installs, no cache restores in deploy jobs, zizmor and `persist-credentials: false` (S00.T16). Actions allowlist and CODEOWNERS on `/.github/` (S00.T15, S00.T17). Separate Cloudflare accounts and least-privilege tokens per environment, rotated if a workflow or action is ever suspect. | S00.T09, S00.T16, S00.T17 | open |
| R-23 | **The development account holds the public domain of the project.** `soldock.org` is the development mail domain, the project homepage and the planned docs site (S14.T01). Email Routing and custom domains need the zone in the account of the Worker. So the development token, which has no reviewer, can change the DNS, the mail and the docs site of the project. The spike (S00.T13), the deliverability suite (S05.T12), dogfooding (S05.T14) and the load tests (S13.T01) send and receive as `@soldock.org`, so a blocklist entry or a spam complaint affects the public domain. | M | H | A maintainer decision (2026-10-18), recorded in ADR-0015. Test mail goes only to seed accounts, inside the Email Service quota. Publish DMARC `p=quarantine` or stricter before the first send. Turn on DNSSEC, and Cloudflare notifications for DNS and Worker route changes, in the development account. S04.T02 decides if the DNS and Email Routing permissions go in a separate token. The S14.T01 kickoff decides if the docs site stays on this zone. | S00.T09, S00.T13, S04.T02, S05.T12, S13.T01, S14.T01 | open |
| R-24 | **Development is both the integration target and the only rehearsal tier.** Each merge to `main` redeploys it. A long run on it can restart in the middle: the 24 h soak (S13.T09), the streaming export (S12.T11), the DR drill (S12.T08), the upgrade rehearsal (S14.T03) and dogfooding (S05.T14). Dogfood data has no backup before S12. | H | M | S05.T25 adds a deploy freeze to `deploy-development.yaml`, and each long run sets it. A long run records the deploys during it, so that its report separates deploy noise from regressions. Dogfood data is disposable, and the dogfood report says so. | S05.T25, S05.T14, S12.T08, S12.T11, S13.T09, S14.T03 | open |
